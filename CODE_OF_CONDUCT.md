# Code of conduct

Ancient Lens is built by volunteers for players of every skill level. Everyone taking part in
issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews is expected to keep it a place where people want
to help.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Keep discussion on topic and accept a maintainer's decision on scope.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal and political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging violence.
- Publishing someone's private information, including Steam or Dota IDs, real names,
  match captures or notes about a player, without their consent.
- Mocking players over their rank, region or skill: the project shows statistics, it does not
  judge people.
- Requests or pull requests for cheating features, after a maintainer has pointed to the
  [fair-play boundary](CONTRIBUTING.md#rules).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and warn or block participants who
break these rules, in public spaces of this repository and when someone represents the project
elsewhere.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or open a
private [security advisory](https://github.com/PavluntiyJ/ancient-lens/security/advisories/new)
if the matter must not be public. Reports are handled confidentially.
